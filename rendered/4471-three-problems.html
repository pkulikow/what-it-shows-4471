The #4471 page says the 412 merged reports are one bug. They are three. Each has its own cause and needs its own fix and its own ticket.

Not one issue. A parser fix alone leaves 38 of the 412 reports open.

  1. parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Tenants that write the day first get day and month flipped. This is the bug the #4471 page describes.
  2. hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint tells them. The form reads it their way. Only form reports. Intake tagged 15 of these 33 as parser, so a parser fix will not help them. The fix is the hint on booking-v3.
  3. one-day: dates move by exactly one day. This is not a month and day flip. The desk notes already read 5 reports this way, but the table on the #4471 page counts them under parser. Two of them point at time zones. The cause is not confirmed.

How the reports were placed

  • The ticket export has repeated rows. One id is one report: 427 rows, 412 reports.
  • Cause comes from the tenant (its locale and booking form), not from intake's browser guess. The rule is in causeOf in causes.ts.
  • One-day moves come from desk-notes.json.

Tickets

One GitHub issue per problem, each with its report count: issue 1 (parser), issue 2 (hint), issue 3 (one-day).

Lessons

  • Merging on a subject line (decision 0001) joined reports that only look alike. Read the body and the tenant before you merge.
  • A count in the title of a ticket hides how many bugs sit under it.
